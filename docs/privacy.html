Privacy

PokerSI · last updated October 10, 2026

PokerSI has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your games stay on your phone, and so does the AI that plays your opponents. The app goes online for one thing only: downloading a model you chose, from Hugging Face.

What stays on your phone

  • Your chips, games, Run mode progress, settings and everything your opponents say. These are stored in the app's private storage and are not sent anywhere.
  • The AI. Opponents are played by a language model running on your device with llama.cpp. No prompt, card or move is sent to a server for the AI to decide.
  • Models you download, kept in the app's storage until you delete them.

PokerSI turns Android's app backup off, so none of this is copied to a cloud backup or carried to a new phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

What goes over the network

Only a model download you start yourself from the Models screen. PokerSI then connects to Hugging Face (huggingface.co and the servers it hands the file off to) and downloads that model file. Like any web server, Hugging Face sees your IP address, the file requested and a standard app user agent, under Hugging Face's privacy policy. PokerSI sends no identifiers, no account and nothing about you or your games.

If you never download a model, PokerSI never goes online: the built-in House Bot plays every seat.

Your own model files

You can load your own GGUF model files from a folder you pick with Android's file picker. PokerSI can then read the files in that folder and nothing else, only to load a model. It does not copy, change or upload them. Removing the folder in PokerSI gives that access back.

Permissions

  • Internet, for model downloads only.
  • Notifications (you are asked first), to show a download's progress while it runs in the background. Saying no only hides the notification.
  • Running a download in the background, so a multi-gigabyte model can finish downloading while you do something else.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-in, ads, analytics, crash-reporting SDKs or third-party trackers.
  • No selling or sharing of data. There is none to share: we never receive any.
  • No real-money gambling. Chips are pretend and cannot be bought or cashed out.

If you installed PokerSI from Google Play, Google may collect crash and performance statistics according to your device's settings and Google's own policies. That happens in Android and Google Play, not in PokerSI.

Children

PokerSI is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. PokerSI is open source, so every change to the app and to this page is public in the GitHub repository.

Contact

Questions or concerns: open an issue on GitHub. Security problems: please report them privately through GitHub security advisories.
