Poker against a table of oddballs, and every one of them is an AI running on your phone. Chunky 8-bit cards on green felt, CRT scanlines and marquee lights: a 90s video poker cabinet with a whole table of characters. Texas Hold'em, Omaha, Draw, Stud, Short Deck and Crazy Pineapple, plus a Run mode that keeps raising the stakes.

THE TABLE TALKS BACK
• Every opponent is a freshly rolled persona with a pixel face, a name and a personality, from timid to reckless. A small language model plays each one, deciding the bet and the trash talk together.
• The AI runs entirely on your phone with llama.cpp. Nothing you play is sent anywhere.
• Each opponent only ever sees what its seat can see. The deck is shuffled fairly and nobody peeks.
• No model yet? House Bot, a built-in opponent, plays every seat with no download at all, and steps in for any AI player that stalls.

SIX GAMES
• Texas Hold'em: two hole cards, five on the board.
• Pot-Limit Omaha: four hole cards, use exactly two.
• Five-Card Draw: tap HOLD on the keepers, like the cabinet at the arcade.
• Seven-Card Stud: up cards, down cards, no board.
• Short Deck: sixes and up, where a flush beats a full house.
• Crazy Pineapple: three hole cards, throw one away after the flop.
• Side pots, all-ins and split pots are all handled for you.

RUN MODE
• Climb a ladder of tables with rising blinds. Bust out and the run is over.
• Boss opponents bring a rule twist to their table, and you have to adapt.
• After each table, pick your next one: a different game, bigger stakes, or a boss.
• Seeded runs you can share and replay, and a daily run that is the same for everyone.

PICK YOUR BRAIN
• Download a small model from a short curated list, picked to play well on phones. Downloads come from Hugging Face, run in the background and show their progress.
• Or point PokerSI at a folder of your own GGUF files and pick any of them.
• Switch models from the Models screen whenever you like.

RETRO ON THE GLASS
• Pixel cards, LED pot counters, chiptune bleeps and a scanline CRT glow.
• A how-to-play screen covers every game and the hand rankings.

NO NONSENSE
Free. No ads. No in-app purchases. No account. No analytics or tracking. The only time PokerSI goes online is to download a model you chose, from Hugging Face. No real money is ever involved: chips are pretend.

The AI opponents need a reasonably recent phone with a few gigabytes of free storage and memory; older phones can still play every game against House Bot. Requires Android 13 or newer.

Pull up a chair, ante up, and see who blinks first.
