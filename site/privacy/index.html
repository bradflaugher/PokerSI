PRIVACY

The fine print, in plain words

Last updated 2026-10-12

Short version: PokerSI collects nothing about you and shares nothing. The AI runs on your phone. The game only goes online to download a model you chose.

PokerSI (package com.bradflaugher.pokersi) is an Android poker game whose opponents are played by an AI model running on your phone. PokerSI itself collects no data about you and shares none. There are no accounts, ads, or analytics, and it never asks you for a rating. The only time it goes online is to download a model you chose from Hugging Face, which sees that request as described under Network below.

What stays on your device

  • Your chips, games, Poker Tour progress, records, settings, and everything your opponents say are stored in app-private storage. Android app backup is turned off, so none of it is copied to the cloud or to a new phone. Clearing the app's data deletes it. When you uninstall, Android may offer to keep the app's data (your downloaded models and saved games) on the phone in case you reinstall; it stays only on your device, and if you decline, or clear it later in Android's settings, it is deleted.
  • The AI. Opponents are played by a language model running on your device with llama.cpp. No prompt, card, or move is sent to a server.
  • Models you download are kept in the app's storage until you delete them.
  • The optional debug console records nothing unless you turn it on, and its log only leaves the app if you copy it yourself.

Network

Only a model download you start yourself from the Models screen. PokerSI then connects to Hugging Face (huggingface.co and the servers it hands the file off to) to download that file. Like any web server, Hugging Face sees your IP address and the file requested, under Hugging Face's privacy policy. PokerSI sends no identifiers and nothing about you or your games. If you never download a model, PokerSI never goes online: the built-in House Bot plays every seat.

Your own model files

You can load your own GGUF model files from a folder you pick with Android's file picker. PokerSI reads that folder only to load a model; it does not copy, change, or upload the files. Removing the folder in PokerSI gives that access back.

Links out

Share hands the game's Google Play link to your device's share sheet. Rate opens the game's page in the Play Store app, or in your browser. Feedback opens a new GitHub issue in your browser, pre-filled with the app version, your Android version, and your device model so a bug report is useful. Those three details are part of the page address, so your browser passes them to GitHub when the page opens. Nothing else is included, and nothing is posted unless you submit the issue yourself; you can edit or remove the details first. Privacy policy and Source code open a web page. Those are other apps and sites, and their own privacy policies apply from there; the game itself sends nothing else.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state, for model downloads only.
  • Notifications (you are asked first), to show a download's progress. Saying no only hides the notification.
  • Foreground service (data sync), so a large model can finish downloading while you do something else.

No gambling

Chips are pretend. They cannot be bought, won, or cashed out for money or anything of value.

Children

PokerSI is not directed at children, and it collects no personal data from anyone.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no cookies, scripts, analytics, or third-party requests; GitHub may log visits to its servers under GitHub's privacy statement.
